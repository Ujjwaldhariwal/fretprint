# Sample credits

Recordings in this folder were taken from https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments, then transcoded to mono 96 kb/s MP3, trimmed, and pitch-measured by scripts/build-samples.mjs.

Original sources (from that project's sample-source-info.txt):

- guitar-acoustic: University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples
- guitar-electric, bass-electric: Karoryfer Samples
- guitar-nylon: Freesound 11573__quartertone__classicalguitar-multisampled
- piano: VSCO 2 Community Edition
